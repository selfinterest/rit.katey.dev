Personal means personal

Your relationship.
Your private space.

Your partner notes and dates stay on your iPhone. Your account stores your name and email separately, so signing in doesn’t upload your private tracker.

The app uses Face ID or your device passcode to protect access. Ellen and account administrators can see account information, but the app doesn’t give them access to your tracker.
