Privacy policy

The short version: your private partner notes and dates stay on your iPhone. We store account information to provide sign-in and manage access. We do not sell personal information, run advertising, or send your tracker notes to AI services.

Who we are

Relationship Intelligence Tracker (“RIT,” “the app”) is developed and operated by Katherine Watson, using Ellen’s relationship tracker as its starting point. In this policy, “we” refers to the people operating RIT. This policy covers the iPhone app and this public website at rit.katey.dev.

For privacy questions or requests, email ellen@katey.dev.

Information we handle

Account information

To create and use an account, you verify an email address and choose a name. Our account service stores your email, chosen name, account identifier, verification status, account creation information, and any access permissions or feature entitlements. Sign-in services may also process technical information such as IP addresses, timestamps, and authentication events to deliver and secure the service.

If a social sign-in option is offered in the version you use, the provider may supply an account identifier, name, email address (including an Apple private relay address), and basic profile information you authorize. We use this information for sign-in and account setup. Social sign-in is not permission to read your contacts, email messages, calendar, or social posts.

Your private tracker

The notes, preferences, occasions, dates, and custom prompts you enter about your partner are stored locally on your iPhone, separately for each app account. They are not uploaded to our account database or made available to Ellen or account administrators.

Your entries can include sensitive personal details if you choose to record them. Only enter information you have permission to keep, and use care when recording details about another person.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. Please avoid sending private tracker entries or sign-in codes when requesting help.

How we use information

We use account and technical information to verify identity, deliver sign-in codes, maintain your profile, manage access, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support or privacy requests. Authorized administrators, including Ellen, can view registered users’ names, email addresses, verification status, and account creation details for account administration.

The app uses your local tracker entries to display, edit, and search your saved details on your device. We do not use them for advertising, profiling by our service, or training AI models. We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Services and sharing

We use service providers to operate RIT:

  • Supabase provides account authentication and stores account profiles and access records. See Supabase’s privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers account emails, including sign-in codes, and processes recipient and delivery information. See Resend’s privacy policy.
  • A sign-in provider you choose, when available, processes your authentication under its own privacy policy. See the policies for Apple, Google, and Meta.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this public website and processes web requests. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Account and support information may be processed in countries other than where you live, depending on the providers involved. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, or secure the service. This does not give us access to tracker content stored only on your device.

Storage and security

The app uses iOS device protection and Face ID or your device passcode to control access to the tracker. Biometric matching is handled by iOS; RIT does not receive or store your facial or fingerprint data. Account sessions are stored using the iOS Keychain.

After you have registered and completed your profile, your saved tracker can be opened offline on the same iPhone. Signing out removes the local sign-in session and closes access to that account’s tracker. It preserves the local tracker so the same account can reopen it later.

Full-tracker iCloud backup and recovery are still in development. The current tracker is local, and its database is excluded from ordinary device backup. Do not rely on it being restored after deleting the app, losing your phone, or changing devices. We will update this policy when cloud backup becomes available.

We use access controls and platform security features to protect information. No system can guarantee complete security.

Retention, your choices, and deletion

You can update your chosen name in the app’s Account screen. You can edit or clear notes and delete occasions in your tracker. Hiding a starter prompt does not delete its saved information; deleting a custom prompt removes its associated notes and dates.

Account information is kept while your account is active, unless you ask us to delete it. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your request and maintain relevant records. Service providers may retain security logs or backup copies for their applicable retention periods. Limited records may be retained where required by law or necessary to address security issues or disputes.

To request account deletion, email ellen@katey.dev from the address associated with your account. Use the subject “RIT account deletion.” We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. We will explain any information that must be retained.

Deleting a server account does not remotely erase a tracker on an offline iPhone. Signing out also does not erase local tracker data. To remove the current version’s local app data from a device, use Delete App in iPhone settings, rather than Offload App. Deleting the app does not delete your server account; request account deletion separately. We cannot retrieve or erase tracker entries from a device we do not control.

This website

This public site has no account form, advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or embedded third-party content. It does not set cookies or use browser local storage. Fonts and images are served with the site.

GitHub Pages may process your IP address and other request information to serve and secure the website. If you follow an external link or email us, the destination service handles that interaction under its own privacy practices.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise those rights, contact ellen@katey.dev. We may need to verify your identity. Where applicable, you may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we process account information to provide the service you request, operational and security information for our legitimate interests in running a reliable service, and information required to meet legal obligations. Optional provider permissions are based on the authorization you give that provider and can be managed through its settings.

Children

RIT is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect account information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

Changes and contact

As the app develops, we may update this policy to reflect changes in features or data handling. The effective date at the top identifies the current policy. We will provide an appropriate notice of material changes.

Privacy contact: Katherine Watson, ellen@katey.dev. For help with the app or an account request, visit Support & deletion.
