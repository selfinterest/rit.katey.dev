A little help

Let’s sort it out.

Questions about the app, your account, or your privacy? Get in touch.

App support

Email ellen@katey.dev with a short description of what happened. Your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version can help us investigate. Please leave out private tracker entries and sign-in codes.

The iPhone app is currently in development. If you are looking for access or have a question about availability, use the same address.

Help signing in

Check your inbox and spam folder for the eight-digit email code. Use the most recent code, or request a new one in the app. First-time registration requires an internet connection.

If you can’t access the email address associated with your account, contact support. Signing in with a different account does not move your existing tracker to that account.

Request account deletion

Send an email from your account’s email address with the subject “RIT account deletion.” We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before deleting its account and profile records.

Email an account deletion request

Your tracker is stored locally. Signing out closes access but keeps its data on that iPhone. To remove the current version’s local app data, choose Delete App in iPhone settings; Offload App keeps data. Deleting the app does not delete the server account, and a server account deletion cannot remotely erase an offline device.

Full-tracker backup and recovery are still in development. Deleting the app can permanently remove your saved notes and dates. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion details.

Privacy questions

Contact ellen@katey.dev for questions, a copy or correction of your account information, or other privacy requests. Our privacy policy explains the information handled by the app and this website.
